Dear Devika,

HAPPY BIRTHDAY, MY LIEGE. ♡

First of all, I hope you realise how much of an honour it is for you to receive a birthday letter from one of your most loyal subjects. Please allow 3–5 business days for the royal court to process your appreciation.

Okay, jokes aside.

It's actually funny to think that all of this started because I put ‘Bye Bye’ by the Backstreet Boys on my Instagram Notes and you decided to text me. Imagine explaining to someone that an Instagram note about a song would eventually lead to all these conversations, all this random lore, and me addressing you as ‘My Liege’.

And of course, we cannot forget your legendary title: Destroyer of Women's Rights. A designation bestowed upon you in the aftermath of our extraordinarily feminist Plus Two English textbook. The historical records are questionable, but the nickname has clearly survived.

Then there was your brief but iconic wingwoman era. Short tenure. Lasting legacy. An achievement that will forever remain in the archives.

But honestly, there's something I wanted to tell you beyond all the jokes.

Thank you for being such a kind and wonderful friend to me.

You've been there for me for a long time, and I don't think I always properly express how much I appreciate that. There have been days when I wasn't feeling my best, and somehow, talking to you could make the day feel a little lighter. You have this genuinely comforting energy, and even an ordinary conversation with you could be something I looked forward to.

I appreciate the way you've helped me, the way you've been there, and all those little moments that might not have seemed like a big deal at the time. They meant something to me.

I'm genuinely glad that you texted me that day. Out of all the random things that could have happened, I'm glad that particular conversation did.

You deserve so much happiness, kindness, and good things in the year ahead. I hope this next chapter brings you wonderful experiences, reasons to laugh until your stomach hurts, people who appreciate you for who you are, and plenty of moments that make you feel as good as you've made other people's days feel.

And please continue being your wonderful self. The royal court requires your services, the wingwoman archives must remain active, and the Destroyer of Women's Rights investigation is still technically open.

Happy birthday once again, Devika.

Thank you for being part of my life, and thank you for being you.

With lots of appreciation, Your loyal subject ♡
